Key Findings

The top five products generate $37,249.11, or 61.0% of total revenue, showing that holiday revenue is concentrated in a small group of proven sellers.

Organic Search generated 385 checkouts from 80,527 pageviews (0.478%), more than three times the 0.124% checkout rate from Paid Social (Meta).

Meta spend totaled $37,200.94, but the largest-spend DA | Sales campaign ($12,917.08) has no reported result, so Q4 budget decisions need stronger attribution before scaling.

Recommendations

  1. Print heavy on the proven top sellers, led by Dwellings of Eldervale Standard, LEGENDARY Upgrade Kit, DELUXE Upgrade Kit, We're Doomed!, and the Wooden Upgrade Tokens.
  2. Reallocate $13,200.94 from weaker or unmeasurable Meta activity toward SEO, email/lifecycle marketing, and controlled community/referral tests while keeping measurable Meta campaigns running.
  3. Launch a Q4 checkout-recovery test: after excluding four July carts with quantities above five, 96 clean abandoned checkouts versus 93 completed July orders imply a 50.8% adjusted abandonment rate.
